Artifact 4 • System Design Concept

Plant Emergency Resource-Locator System

A location-based concept for helping safety and facilities teams find critical plant resources quickly during an emergency.

← Back to All Projects

Focus

Safety + Location + Response

Builds on numbered facility columns employees already use as a shared emergency-location reference.

Introduction

Making emergency locations easier to communicate.

During a plant emergency, employees need to communicate the location quickly and responders need immediate access to information about nearby hazards and emergency resources.

The proposed system would allow authorized users to locate fire extinguishers, electrical equipment, water supplies, piping, and other emergency resources near a reported column location.

Objective

Improve emergency coordination

Improve emergency communication and help facilities and safety teams find the correct resources quickly.

Unique Value

Uses an existing location language

The design builds on a location system employees already use, making the concept easier to understand and potentially faster to adopt.

Process

  1. Identified the existing column-number system as the shared location reference.
  2. Defined resource categories such as fire protection, electrical systems, and water infrastructure.
  3. Outlined a searchable location and resource database.
  4. Considered map, QR-code, mobile, and dashboard interfaces.
  5. Identified the need for controlled access, routine verification, and emergency-procedure approval.

Tools & Technology

Process mapping, database and data-field planning, emergency-response workflow design, AI-assisted concept development, and planned dashboard and mapping technologies.

Relevance

Accurate, accessible facility information can support faster coordination during emergencies. The system would supplement—not replace—approved emergency procedures, training, alarms, and professional responders.

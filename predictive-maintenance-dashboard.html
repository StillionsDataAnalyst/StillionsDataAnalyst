Responsible-use note

This dashboard reports results from synthetic demonstration data. It is not a production maintenance decision system. A real deployment would require approved plant data, time-aware validation, leakage checks, threshold tuning, calibration, subject-matter expert review, drift monitoring, and clear operational response procedures.
